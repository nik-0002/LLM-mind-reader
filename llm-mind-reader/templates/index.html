How it works
Three steps, all in activation space
1
Contrast

Run paired prompts (honest vs. deceptive) and subtract their hidden states.

2
Isolate

PCA (or difference of means) finds one direction; its typical gap sets the scale.

3
Inject

Add coefficient × scale × direction to the residual stream while generating.
